FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my data safe?

Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2, connections use HTTPS, sessions use secure HTTP-only cookies, and your invoice data is encrypted at rest with AES-256. Versioned backups can be restored from Settings. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I use it on multiple devices?

Yes. Changes sync to your account automatically. If the same account is edited on two devices, InvoiceFlow detects the conflict and merges the changes record by record, keeping the most recent edit of each.

Does it work offline?

Once you're signed in, edits are saved on your device first and synced when your connection returns. Signing in requires a connection.

Which taxes and currencies are supported?

You can set any tax label (GST, VAT, Sales Tax) and per-line rates, in INR, USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, AUD, CAD, SGD or AED. InvoiceFlow does not file taxes or generate government e-invoices (such as IRN or e-way bills) — please confirm your local invoicing requirements with your accountant.

Can I export my data?

Any time. Export invoices to CSV or your full account to JSON from Settings → Data & backups.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account, invoices, clients and backups are permanently erased immediately. You can also email a deletion request to the contact in our Privacy Policy.
