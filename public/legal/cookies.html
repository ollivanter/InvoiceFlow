Cookie Policy

Effective

Short version. We use one strictly necessary cookie to keep you signed in, plus your browser's local storage for preferences and unsynced edits. We do not use advertising, analytics, social-media or other tracking cookies, and we do not load third-party scripts that set cookies. Strictly necessary storage doesn't require consent, so the notice we show is for information only — there is nothing to switch off.

Optional bot protection

If Cloudflare Turnstile is enabled on the sign-in and sign-up forms, Cloudflare may process technical data, such as your IP address and browser characteristics, to tell people apart from bots. It is used only for that security purpose and not for advertising.

Managing storage

You can delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings at any time. If you block the session cookie, you won't be able to stay signed in.
