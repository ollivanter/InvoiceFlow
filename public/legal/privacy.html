3. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account, verify your email, and keep you signed in.
  • To store, sync and back up your invoices and business data across your devices.
  • To send security emails you request, such as verification and password-reset codes.
  • To send product update emails — only if you opted in. You can opt out at any time.
  • To prevent abuse, such as brute-force sign-in attempts and automated sign-ups.

We do not sell or rent personal data, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions that have legal effects on you.

4. Legal bases

Where applicable law (such as the GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) requires a legal basis, we rely on: performance of a contract to provide the service; legitimate interests for security and fraud prevention; consent for optional product emails, which you can withdraw at any time; and legal obligations where the law requires us to keep or disclose data.

6. Retention

  • Account and business data: kept while your account is active and deleted immediately when you delete your account.
  • Backups: up to the 30 most recent automatic and manual snapshots of your data. They are deleted with your account.
  • Sessions: expire after 30 days or when you sign out.
  • Verification codes: expire after 10 minutes and are deleted after use.
  • Rate-limit records: deleted shortly after the 15–60 minute window they cover.
  • Hosting provider logs: retained by our hosting provider for a limited period under its own policy.

7. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA512 and a unique salt. Your business data and backups are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, and database row-level security restricts each account to its own records. Sign-in, verification and password reset are rate limited. No system is perfectly secure; if we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

8. Your rights and data deletion

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to object to or restrict certain processing.

  • Access & export: Settings → Data & backups → Export all data downloads a complete JSON copy of your account and data.
  • Correction: edit your details at any time in Settings.
  • Deletion: Settings → Account → Delete account permanently erases your account, data, backups and sessions immediately.
  • Email requests: if you cannot sign in, email from the address on your account with the subject “Data deletion request” (or “Data access request”). We may ask you to verify ownership of the account, and we respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The service is a business tool and is not directed at children. You must be at least years old to create an account, and we ask you to confirm this at sign-up. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact and we will delete it.

10. Emails

We send security emails, such as verification and password-reset codes, only when you request them. Product update emails are sent only if you opt in. Every email includes our business details and an unsubscribe link, and you can change your preference in Settings → Account.

11. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. For material changes, we will notify you by email or in the app before they take effect.
